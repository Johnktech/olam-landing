Uganda's soil is among the richest on earth. Our skin deserves to know that.

Olam was born from a simple frustration: why were Ugandans buying imported shower products when our land holds some of the world's finest botanicals?

We spent two years working with smallholder farmers across Uganda's regions — from Acholi shea harvesters to Mbale aloe growers — building supply chains that pay fairly and deliver exceptional raw materials.

Every bottle of Olam is a celebration of Ugandan biodiversity, formulated with the precision of luxury and the soul of home.
